Auto Edit settings

🎥 Multi-cam: import clips of the same moment shot from different phones/angles. They're lined up by their sound (to the millisecond), then the edit cuts between the angles, picking the sharpest, steadiest one, with one continuous sound track. Fix the sync or the grouping in the Multi-cam box after an Auto Edit; pick the camera for any shot in the Clip tab.

Extend uses only your footage: slow motion on the sharpest, steadiest moments, boomerang loops, and replays of the best moments from another angle.

🎯 Tip: Clip tab → Set focus, tap her on the preview, and every crop/zoom follows that spot.

Analysis runs at low resolution on-device. Color/zoom preview live; denoise, sharpen, stabilize and crossfades are applied in the exported MP4. For the cloud upscaler, use AI ✨ → Enhance after exporting.
